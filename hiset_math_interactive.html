Algebraic concepts

Lines and slope: y = mx + b

Slope tells you how steep the line is. The y-intercept tells you where it crosses the y-axis.

What to notice: A positive slope rises left to right and a negative slope falls. Slope 0 is a flat line. Changing b slides the whole line up or down without changing its steepness.

Measurement and geometry

The Pythagorean theorem

In a right triangle, the squares on the two legs add up to the square on the hypotenuse: a² + b² = c².

What to notice: The two small squares always cover exactly the same area as the big one. Whole-number sets like 3-4-5 and 5-12-13 show up on tests often, and multiples of them (6-8-10) work too.

Numbers and operations

Fractions, decimals, and percents

They are three names for the same amount. Pick a fraction and see all three.

What to notice: Percent is just "out of 100." To change a fraction to a percent, divide the top by the bottom and multiply by 100. Memorize the friendly ones: 1/2 = 50%, 1/4 = 25%, 1/5 = 20%, 1/8 = 12.5%.

Data analysis, probability, and statistics

Mean, median, mode, and range

Change any of the five values and watch the measures move. The median is always the middle of the ordered list.

What to notice: Try pushing one value far to the right. The mean follows it, but the median barely moves. That is why a few very large values pull an average up.
